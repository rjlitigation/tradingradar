# Refund Policy

**Platform:** TradingRadar

---

## 1. General Rule
- All subscriptions are **non-refundable** once activated.  

---

## 2. Exceptions
Refunds shall be issued only in the following circumstances:  
1. Duplicate payments.  
2. Transaction failure where funds are debited but subscription not activated.  
3. Proven technical failure solely attributable to TradingRadar.  

---

## 3. Refund Process
- Refunds, if approved, are credited **exclusively** to the original mode of payment.  
- Processing period: **nine (9) to fourteen (14) working days** from approval.  

---

## 4. Exclusions
No refund shall be granted for:  
- User dissatisfaction or change of mind.  
- Service interruptions caused by internet outage, force majeure, or third-party downtime.  
- Misuse or unauthorized use of User credentials.  

---

## 5. Finality
All refund decisions made by TradingRadar are final and binding.
