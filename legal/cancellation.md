# Cancellation Policy

**Platform:** TradingRadar

---

## 1. Automatic Expiry
Subscriptions terminate automatically upon expiry of the prepaid term.  

---

## 2. Mid-Term Cancellation
- Users cannot cancel subscriptions mid-term.  
- No pro-rata or partial refunds are permitted.  

---

## 3. Non-Renewal
- Subscriptions shall cease automatically if not renewed.  

---

## 4. Non-Transferability
- Subscriptions cannot be transferred, resold, or sublicensed.  

---

## 5. Finality
- Cancellation is deemed complete upon expiry. No reinstatement without new subscription.
